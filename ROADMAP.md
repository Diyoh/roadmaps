# AI for Everyday Work: a free-first roadmap

Six levels, about 490 hours at 2 hours a day, from **2 Nov 2026** to **4 Jul 2027**. It teaches you to use AI for writing, graphics, video, websites, business operations and ads, and to turn that into paid work. Every phase has a **free route** (US$0) and a **paid route** (what to buy, in what order, and when it is worth it).

It follows the format of the `ai-engineer` roadmap on the Roadmaps platform: levels, dated phases, "exams" (here: free certificates and mentor-reviewed checkpoints), qualifications, community and a milestone checklist. The same content is in `public/index.html`.

## How the budget routes work

| Route | Monthly cost | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| A: free only | US$0 | Free tiers of ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, Canva Free, CapCut or DaVinci Resolve, Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages, Make or Activepieces free plans, HubSpot free CRM, Zoho Invoice, free certificates |
| B: one upgrade | about US$10–25 | One assistant plan (Gemini AI Plus US$4.99, ChatGPT Go US$8, or about US$20 for ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro or Gemini AI Pro) plus one specialist tool (for example ElevenLabs Starter US$6 or Canva Pro about US$12) |
| C: builder | about US$40–70 | Adds an app builder or coding plan (Lovable or Bolt about US$25, Replit about US$20–25) and an automation plan (Make Core US$9) |
| D: heavy use | US$100 and up | Top-tier assistant plans (for example Claude Max or ChatGPT Pro from about US$100). Only once client income pays for it |

**Rule of thumb:** stay on route A until a real job is blocked by a free limit, move to B for one tool at a time, and upgrade only when the tool saves at least its cost within two months or lets you deliver paid work.

## Level 1: AI foundations and your free toolkit

**2 Nov 2026 – 29 Nov 2026 · 56 hours.** Use an AI assistant well every day, build a safe free toolkit, know every tool’s free limits, and choose one venture project to carry through all six levels.

### 2 – 8 Nov

Set up. Free route: open free accounts on ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude and use all three for a week. Their free limits differ: ChatGPT offers everyday text chat and limited image generation; Claude resets its allowance every 5 hours and has no image generation; Gemini also refreshes every 5 hours and includes image generation and Deep Research. Add GitHub, a Google account, Canva Free and NotebookLM. Start a prompt log (a Google Doc or sheet) and learn one prompt pattern: role, goal, context, format, a worked example, then a check. Paid route: pay for nothing this week. Write down every time you hit a limit; that list shows what is worth buying. Cheapest upgrades: Gemini AI Plus US$4.99 a month, ChatGPT Go US$8, or about US$20 for ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro or Gemini AI Pro.

[ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) · [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) · [Claude](https://claude.ai/) · [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com/) · [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/)

### 9 – 15 Nov

Everyday tasks. Use AI for jobs you already do: emails and messages, summaries of long documents, research with sources, meeting notes, translation, spreadsheet formulas, study plans and scripts. The rule: AI drafts, you decide, and you check every fact, number and link before it leaves your hands. Free route: the three free assistants, NotebookLM for questions over your own documents, and Google AI Studio for free experiments with newer models. Paid route: none yet. Take the OpenAI Academy course AI Foundations (free, with a completion certificate and badge) and finish it by 15 Nov 2026.

[OpenAI Academy](https://academy.openai.com/) · [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/) · [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com/) · [Elements of AI](https://www.elementsofai.com/)

### 16 – 22 Nov

Safety, limits and costs. Never paste passwords, client secrets, ID numbers or private customer data into a free chat tool, and check each tool’s data-use setting. Free plans often come with licence rules: ElevenLabs’ free plan is non-commercial, Vercel’s Hobby plan is for personal use only, and Luma’s free use is non-commercial, so read the licence before client work. Build your tool sheet (a spreadsheet): tool, free limit, when it resets, watermark, commercial rights, paid price, date checked. Free route: run a small open model on your own computer with Ollama if it has about 16 GB of RAM; otherwise skip. Paid route: pay-per-token APIs only when something you build needs one. Free API tiers exist (Google AI Studio; DeepSeek and Z.ai have offered free tokens), but limits change, so check them.

[Ollama](https://ollama.com/) · [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/) · [DeepSeek platform](https://platform.deepseek.com/) · [Z.ai](https://z.ai/)

### 23 – 29 Nov

Choose your venture project. Pick one real or realistic small business to carry through all six levels: your own idea, a family shop, a local business or a niche service. Write a one-page brief with AI as a sparring partner (who it serves, what it sells, the price, why people would care), then talk to three real people to test it, because AI cannot replace real customers. Finish the Google Skills path Introduction to Generative AI (about 2 hours, free badge) by 29 Nov 2026. Free route: everything in this level costs nothing. Paid route: not needed. Post your Day 1 story and share your tool sheet with your study group.

[Google Skills: Introduction to Generative AI](https://www.skills.google/paths/118) · [Lean Canvas](https://leanstack.com/lean-canvas) · [Coursera Financial Aid](https://www.coursera.org/about/financial-aid)

> Weekly rhythm: Monday and Tuesday to learn (course or tool tutorial), Wednesday for a practice drill, Thursday to compare one task in two tools, Friday and Saturday for the venture project, Sunday to publish and review. Budget routes used throughout: Route A US$0 (free tiers only); Route B about US$10–25 a month (one assistant plan plus one specialist tool); Route C about US$40–70 a month (adds a builder or automation plan); Route D US$100 and up, only once clients pay for it. Free limits quoted in this roadmap come from third-party roundups checked in October 2026 and change often, so confirm each one on the vendor’s own page before relying on it.

## Level 2: Graphics, brand and writing

**30 Nov 2026 – 3 Jan 2027 · 70 hours.** Design a complete brand and a month of content with free tools, learn which paid design tools are worth buying, and publish your first portfolio set.

### 30 Nov – 6 Dec

Design basics and brand kit. Learn contrast, hierarchy, spacing, two fonts and a small colour palette, then build a brand kit for your venture: logo, colours, fonts and voice. Free route: Canva Free (its AI tools include a shared monthly pool of roughly 200 standard or 20 premium uses, plus background remover and Magic Write), Adobe Express’s free tier and Autodraw for quick icons. One review says free Canva AI images carry commercial rights and another says they do not, so read Canva’s current licence before client work. Paid route: Canva Pro, about US$12 a month (US$144 a year), or Adobe Express or Firefly from US$9.99 a month. Stay on Canva Free until a real limit stops you.

[Canva](https://www.canva.com/) · [Adobe Express](https://www.adobe.com/express/) · [Autodraw](https://www.autodraw.com/) · [Canva Design School](https://www.canva.com/designschool/)

### 7 – 20 Dec

AI images and logos. Free route: Gemini’s image generation (free, no fixed count), Reve (reported at 20 images a day), Leonardo (about 150 tokens a day, roughly 18–30 images), Ideogram (best at text inside images) and Recraft (vector and SVG output) all have free tiers. Check each tool’s licence and watermark rules. Practise product mock-ups, social backgrounds, icons, and a logo concept that you rebuild cleanly in Canva or Recraft. Do not imitate living artists or brands. Paid route: Ideogram from US$8 a month, Leonardo or Recraft from US$12, Midjourney from US$10 (no free tier). Buy at most one image tool, and only after the free tiers stop you finishing a real job.

[Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) · [Ideogram](https://ideogram.ai/) · [Leonardo](https://leonardo.ai/) · [Recraft](https://www.recraft.ai/) · [Midjourney](https://www.midjourney.com/)

### 21 Dec – 3 Jan

Writing and brand voice. A lighter holiday pace is fine. Write a brand voice guide, then use AI as an editor rather than an author: product descriptions, a 12-post social calendar, a welcome email, a five-slide pitch and a one-page flyer, all in your own voice. Check for invented facts and for text that sounds like everyone else. Free route: the free assistants, NotebookLM and Canva presentations. Paid route: one US$20 assistant plan, only if you write for clients every week. Finish Google AI Essentials (about 5–10 hours; audit it free) by 27 Dec 2026, add Google’s AI for Small Businesses lessons, and publish your first portfolio page with the brand kit and 12 posts by 3 Jan 2027.

[Google AI Essentials and AI for Small Businesses](https://grow.google/ai/) · [Google Skills](https://www.skills.google/paths/2336) · [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com/)

> Designs sell through clarity, not novelty. Free tiers reset daily or monthly, so generate in batches on the days your credits refresh and write down which prompts worked. Free AI images may carry licence limits or watermarks: record them in the tool sheet before anything goes to a client.

## Level 3: Video, voice and short-form content

**4 Jan 2027 – 14 Feb 2027 · 84 hours.** Script, record, edit and publish short videos with free tools, add AI voice and clips only where they help, and know which paid tool earns its monthly fee.

### 4 – 17 Jan

Script to edit. Learn the pipeline: a hook in the first 3 seconds, one idea, captions and a call to action. Write scripts with a free assistant, film on your phone, then edit. Free route: CapCut (free, 1080p export; auto-captions are capped at 10 minutes per video), DaVinci Resolve (free, no watermark, up to 4K UHD; the Studio upgrade is a one-time US$295) and Clipchamp (free, 1080p, no watermark). Paid route: Descript Hobbyist at US$24 a month for transcript-style editing (its free plan is one watermarked 720p video a month), or Opus Clip at US$15 a month to cut long videos into clips. Skip both until you edit every week.

[CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/) · [DaVinci Resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve) · [Clipchamp](https://clipchamp.com/) · [Descript](https://www.descript.com/) · [Opus Clip](https://www.opus.pro/)

### 18 – 31 Jan

Voice and sound. Record your own voice first, with a phone microphone in a quiet room. Add AI voice-over only where you cannot record. Free route: ElevenLabs’ free plan gives 10,000 credits a month (about 10 minutes of speech), is non-commercial, requires attribution and does not roll credits over, so use it for practice only; Fish Audio has been reported to include 8,000 free credits a month. Paid route: ElevenLabs Starter at US$6 a month (US$5 billed yearly) gives about 30 minutes and commercial use for audio made while subscribed. Never clone someone’s voice without their written permission. Make a 45-second voice-over ad and a captioned explainer.

[ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/) · [DaVinci Resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve)

### 1 – 14 Feb

AI video clips, used sparingly. Free route: tools that renew free credits, such as Google Flow (about 50 credits a day), Kling (66 credits a day, roughly two watermarked 720p clips of 5 seconds), PixVerse (about 30–60 credits a day) and Krea (100 compute units a day). Runway gives a one-time 125 credits and Pika about 80 a month. Watermark and commercial rules differ, so check them before client use. Paid route: Google AI Plus at US$4.99 a month adds Flow credits; Pika Creator at US$35, Luma from US$30 and Runway from about US$15 come next. Sora has been shut down, so do not build on it. Use AI clips as B-roll or product shots, mix them with real footage and captions, and follow each platform’s rules on labelling AI-generated content. Publish eight short videos and submit Checkpoint 3 by 14 Feb 2027.

[Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) · [Kling](https://klingai.com/) · [PixVerse](https://pixverse.ai/) · [Krea](https://www.krea.ai/) · [Pika](https://pika.art/) · [Runway](https://runwayml.com/)

> Video is the most expensive thing to generate and the easiest to waste. Use one hero idea a week, reuse it across formats, and treat paid video credits as the last thing you buy.

**You should hold:** Eight published short videos with captions; A 30-second product ad and a voice-over explainer; A one-page note on which video and voice tools passed your licence check.

## Level 4: Websites, apps and automation

**15 Feb 2027 – 11 Apr 2027 · 112 hours.** Ship a real website for your venture with AI help, add forms, payments or a chatbot, and run three automations that save you hours every week.

### 15 – 28 Feb

Web basics with an AI tutor. Learn how a page works (HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript), what a domain, hosting and a repository are, and how to read the code an AI writes before you trust it. Work through freeCodeCamp’s free web design lessons beside an assistant that explains each error. Free route: VS Code with GitHub Copilot Free (about 2,000 completions and 50 chat requests a month), Cursor Hobby (about 2,000 tab completions and 50 premium requests a month), Windsurf (25 Cascade credits a month) or Gemini CLI (reported at 1,000 requests a day, but Google has been moving individual users to Antigravity, so check). Paid route: a US$20 assistant plan, or Replit Core at about US$20–25 a month, once free limits block you every week.

[freeCodeCamp](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/) · [VS Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/) · [GitHub Copilot](https://github.com/features/copilot) · [Cursor](https://cursor.com/) · [Windsurf](https://windsurf.com/) · [Gemini CLI](https://github.com/google-gemini/gemini-cli)

### 1 – 21 Mar

Build the site. Sketch with a builder, then own the code. Free route: Lovable (5 credits a day, up to 30 a month), Bolt (about 300K tokens a day), v0 (7 messages a day), Base44 (25 message credits a month, up to 5 apps) and Google AI Studio (up to 2 apps deployed free) give you a first draft. Push it to GitHub, then refine it by hand and with a coding assistant. Host static sites on Cloudflare Pages (unlimited static bandwidth, commercial use allowed), GitHub Pages or Netlify’s free plan. Vercel’s Hobby plan is non-commercial, so do not run a business site on it. Paid route: Lovable or Bolt Pro at about US$25 a month, Replit Core, and a custom domain (usually around US$10–15 a year). Test on a phone, on slow data and in two browsers.

[Lovable](https://lovable.dev/) · [Bolt](https://bolt.new/) · [v0](https://v0.dev/) · [Base44](https://base44.com/) · [Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com/) · [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/) · [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/)

### 22 Mar – 4 Apr

Add features. Add a contact form, booking or payment link, analytics, SEO basics, a simple database and sign-in (Supabase and Firebase both have free tiers; check their limits), and an AI chat helper that answers from your own FAQ (Tidio’s free plan has included 50 AI conversations a month; Chatbase and Crisp also have free plans). Take payments with a payment link or Stripe; Stripe Invoicing has included 25 free invoices. Security basics: never put API keys in your repository, review every line an AI writes for forms and payments, and add a privacy page. If you want to build with APIs, take an Anthropic Academy course (free, with completion certificates; its Claude API course is 8+ hours of video) by 4 Apr 2027. Paid route: pay-as-you-go API credit only if your chat helper needs it.

[Supabase](https://supabase.com/) · [Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/) · [Tidio](https://www.tidio.com/) · [Stripe](https://stripe.com/) · [Anthropic Academy](https://anthropic.skilljar.com/)

### 5 – 11 Apr

Automation. Free route: Make’s free plan (1,000 operations a month), Zapier’s (100 tasks a month, 5 Zaps), Activepieces (1,000 tasks a month in the cloud, unlimited self-hosted) or self-hosted n8n (free software; a small server costs roughly US$5–20 a month). Build three automations: a form that writes a row to a sheet and emails you, a weekly summary report, and a content repurposing flow. Paid route: Make Core at US$9 a month (10,000 operations), Zapier Starter at about US$20, n8n Cloud at US$24. Finish the OpenAI Academy course Agents and Workflows by 9 Apr 2027, then submit Checkpoint 4 by 11 Apr 2027.

[Make](https://www.make.com/) · [Zapier](https://zapier.com/) · [n8n](https://n8n.io/) · [Activepieces](https://www.activepieces.com/) · [OpenAI Academy](https://academy.openai.com/)

> AI-written code looks right even when it is wrong. Read it, run it, test it, and keep every project in GitHub so you can undo mistakes. Free hosting tiers differ on commercial use and on what happens when you pass a limit: on some, every project on the account pauses.

## Level 5: Business operations, ads and sales

**12 Apr 2027 – 23 May 2027 · 84 hours.** Run the business side of your venture with free tools, create and test ads with discipline, and earn the free marketing certificates that clients recognise.

### 12 – 25 Apr

Operations. Turn the venture brief into a lean canvas, then set up the systems that stop work falling through the cracks. Free route: HubSpot’s free CRM for contacts and deals; Google Sheets as your dashboard; invoicing with Zoho Invoice (free, unlimited), Wave’s free Starter plan, Invoice Ninja’s free plan (5 clients) or Stripe Invoicing; email with the free plans of Brevo (300 emails a day), MailerLite (1,000 subscribers) or Mailchimp (500 contacts); and a free support chatbot. Use AI to draft SOPs, proposals and contracts, then have a local expert check anything legal or tax-related. Paid route: Wave Pro US$19 a month, Invoice Ninja Pro US$14, Make Core US$9, or a business assistant plan from about US$20. Finish OpenAI Academy’s Applied AI Foundations by 25 Apr 2027.

[HubSpot free CRM](https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm) · [Zoho Invoice](https://www.zoho.com/invoice/) · [Wave](https://www.waveapps.com/) · [Invoice Ninja](https://invoiceninja.com/) · [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/) · [MailerLite](https://www.mailerlite.com/) · [OpenAI Academy](https://academy.openai.com/)

### 26 Apr – 9 May

Ads: research and creative. Before spending a cent, study what already works: Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency Center and TikTok Top Ads are free. Free route: platform tools such as Meta’s text variations (up to 5 per ad), Google Asset Studio and TikTok Symphony Creative Studio, plus your own Canva and video skills, and free courses at Google Skillshop, Meta Blueprint and HubSpot Academy. Write three offers with three hooks each, build a landing page with one clear action, and set up Google Analytics so you can measure. Paid route: ad spend. Third-party guides suggest budgets near US$20 a day for Google’s AI Max and US$50 a day for Meta Advantage+ or TikTok’s automated campaigns, but you can test far smaller; Google’s Smart Bidding wants about 30 conversions before it can optimise. Finish the HubSpot Digital Marketing certification (free, about 3 hours) by 9 May 2027.

[Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/) · [Google Ads Transparency Center](https://adstransparency.google.com/) · [TikTok Creative Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/) · [Google Skillshop](https://skillshop.withgoogle.com/) · [Meta Blueprint](https://www.facebook.com/business/learn) · [HubSpot Academy](https://academy.hubspot.com/) · [Google Analytics](https://analytics.google.com/)

### 10 – 23 May

Run a controlled test. If you have a budget, spend a small learning amount on one platform with a single objective and tracking set up first, and write down the result before you change anything. If you have no budget, build a complete campaign pack for a real local business (ad creatives, copy, landing page, tracking plan and a measurement sheet) and offer it as a free case study. Use AI to analyse results, but check the numbers yourself. Follow each platform’s rules on AI-generated content, health and finance claims, and before-and-after images. Pass the Google Ads certification at Skillshop (free exam, about 75 minutes, about 80% to pass, valid for 12 months) by 16 May 2027. Optional paid step: the Meta Certified Digital Marketing Associate exam (US$99; courses and practice exams are free) by 23 May 2027.

[Google Skillshop](https://skillshop.withgoogle.com/) · [Meta Blueprint](https://www.facebook.com/business/learn) · [Google Meridian (free measurement)](https://github.com/google/meridian)

> Ads multiply whatever you already have: a good offer gets better and a weak offer gets expensive. Do not spend until tracking works and the landing page loads quickly on a phone. Spend only money you can afford to lose, treat month one as paid learning, and never let an AI tool raise a budget without your approval.

## Level 6: Launch: portfolio, clients and first income

**24 May 2027 – 4 Jul 2027 · 84 hours.** Package what you can now do into two or three priced offers, win your first paying customers, and decide with data which tools to pay for.

### 24 May – 6 Jun

Package your offers. Choose two or three things you can deliver reliably: a social-content pack, a landing page in five days, ad creatives and setup, or business automation. Price each by the hours it takes and the value it gives, set a scope, a turnaround time and a revision limit, and write a simple agreement. Build a portfolio site (reuse Level 4) with six case studies: the problem, the tools used, the time and cost saved, and the result. Show your process honestly, including what AI did and what you checked. Free route: your Level 4 site, Canva, Google Docs and Stripe or a local payment method. Paid route: a custom domain and one assistant plan. Submit Checkpoint 5 by 6 Jun 2027.

[Stripe](https://stripe.com/) · [Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com/) · [Canva](https://www.canva.com/)

### 7 – 20 Jun

Find first customers. Pre-sell before you build: message five local businesses or niche communities a day with a 60-second demo video, not a long pitch.
