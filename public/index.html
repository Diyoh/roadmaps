    Adding students

    1. Send students the link to this site. They sign in with Google, or with an email and password.
    2. They read and sign the commitment with their full name, then wait for you.
    3. They appear under Waiting for approval at the top of this page. Approve the students you know; remove anyone you don’t. Only approved students can use the plan.

    Assign a task

    Tasks appear on each chosen student’s “My assignments” page. Grade them from the student’s page in Class.

    Holidays
